L4D2 8-PLAYER CO-OP - JOIN APP
==============================

This app joins a friend's 8-player Left 4 Dead 2 game.
You don't need any mods, and it doesn't change your game.

HOW TO JOIN
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (Desktop is fine).
2. Open L4D2-8P-Join.exe.
3. Paste the invite your host sent you
   (it looks like steam://connect/1.2.3.4:27015).
4. Click "Check server", then "Join game".
   L4D2 starts and connects you automatically.
   If you end up spectating, type !join in chat.

"WINDOWS PROTECTED YOUR PC"?
The app isn't code-signed, so Windows may warn you the first time.
Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

OPTIONAL: 8-PLAYER HUD
The link at the bottom of the app installs a small HUD add-on.
It shows the extra survivors with health bars and item icons
(without it you'd see plain text boxes, if your host uses that look).
Close L4D2 before installing it. You can remove it from the same link.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Needs Windows 10 or 11 and Steam with Left 4 Dead 2 installed.
- If the host plays a custom campaign, "Check server" tells you
  and links to it on the Workshop.
- Playing on the same home network? Use "Find games on my network".
- The app remembers the servers you've joined under "Recent groups".
- Settings are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\L4D2-8P-Manager.
  Delete that folder to reset them.

The licenses folder covers the fonts built into the app.
